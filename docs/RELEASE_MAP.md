## Store reality
- iOS App Store: supported route after Family Controls distribution approval and App Review.
- iOS direct web installation: region/platform limited; not a universal India distribution method.
- Google Play: publish the product mode that meets the current Families/location/payment rules.
- Android direct APK: appropriate for private/managed distribution; use signed production artifacts only.

## No claim in this source bundle
No one should call this "certified for sale" until Apple/Google review, telecom/privacy/legal requirements and real-device testing are completed.
